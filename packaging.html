Folding Carton Packaging

Folding cartons are designed around your product, from the material and structural style to the way the package opens, closes, displays, and protects what's inside. Print NW helps you determine the right combination for your product, production needs, and customer experience.

Materials & Construction

Print NW manufactures printed product packaging, not the conventional corrugated boxes your product is shipped in. Both paperboard and litho-laminated corrugated are designed for packaging where appearance and brand presentation matter. The right construction depends on your product and what the package needs to do. Some products are best suited to paperboard packaging, while others need the added strength and structure of litho-laminated corrugated.

Paperboard

A sturdy packaging material that can be printed on, die cut, scored, folded, and formed into custom packaging. Paperboard is available in a variety of thicknesses, grades, and finishes to meet different product and packaging needs.

Litho-Laminated Corrugated

A printed top sheet is laminated to corrugated board to combine high-quality custom printing with added strength and structure. While traditional litho-lamination uses an offset-printed top sheet, Print NW can also produce digitally printed top sheets for this type of packaging.
