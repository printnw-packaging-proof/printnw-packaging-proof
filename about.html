Packaging is a Team Sport

We believe in collaboration.

The best packaging projects don't happen because one person has all the answers. They happen because people with different perspectives come together around the same goal.

At Print NW, collaboration isn't something we schedule. It's part of how we work every day. We ask each other questions. We share ideas across departments. We challenge assumptions. Sometimes the best solution comes from someone you didn't expect.

It's a culture built on trust, where everyone has something valuable to contribute. That's the kind of environment where collaboration thrives and great work begins.

Experience Changes the Conversation

We value wisdom.

Experience isn't just measured in years. It's measured in the thousands of projects that teach you what works, what doesn't, and what to look for next time.

Many of the people at Print NW have spent years growing alongside one another. They've watched technology evolve, solved complex manufacturing challenges, and learned from every challenge along the way. That shared experience creates a level of trust and perspective that can't be rushed.

When you've worked together for a long time, conversations become more thoughtful. You listen differently. You notice things others might miss. And you make better decisions because you've been there before.

Better Questions Lead to Better Packaging

We value curiosity.

One of the most valuable things we can bring to a project isn't an answer. It's a question.

Curiosity keeps us learning. It encourages us to look beyond the obvious, explore new ideas, and think through the details before they become challenges. Sometimes that means asking a few extra questions. Sometimes it means looking at a project from a different perspective.

We're not interested in making assumptions. We'd rather have thoughtful conversations early, because that's often where the best ideas begin.

Built by People Who Care

We take pride in our work.

There's something special about working alongside people who genuinely care. They care about the product, the people they work beside, and the customers they serve.

Pride isn't something you turn on at the end of a project. It's woven into the work itself.

Every package that leaves our facility represents hundreds of decisions, countless details, and the collective effort of people who care deeply about getting it right.

Our Roots

Print NW opened in 2001 in a small building in downtown Puyallup. We may have been a modest shop with limited equipment, but it was full of people determined to build something great.

Today, we're proud to be the largest commercial printer in the South Sound, with more than 100 employees working across two facilities totaling over 60,000 square feet in Lakewood, Washington.

From our Lakewood facilities in the Tacoma/Seattle area, we work with packaging customers throughout the Pacific Northwest and across the western U.S.

We're proud of how far we've come, and even more excited about where we're headed.
